On your timeline in three steps.

Griddy works like any built-in transition, so there's nothing new to learn.

  1. Install it

    Run the installer and restart Premiere. On a Mac it doesn't even ask for your password.

  2. Drag it onto a cut

    It's in Video Transitions › Artzen Media. It also works on the start or end of a single clip.

  3. Pick a preset

    Choose one of 13, or hit Surprise Me for a new look on every click. Drag the transition's edges to change its length.

Grid

Columns, rows, square cells, the order the cells cut in, and timing.

Tiles Pro

Gaps between cells, rounded corners and the colour behind them.

Lines

Colour, thickness, opacity, softness and glow. Thickness scales with the frame.

Grid animation

11 ways for the lines to come in and go out, like a fade, a draw-on or lines growing out of their crossings.

Flash Pro

A flat colour hold before each cell cuts, or a quick flash as it does.

Keep the grid up between cuts.

Griddy comes with Griddy Lines, the same grid as an effect. Put it on an adjustment layer over a whole section and the lines stay on screen while the cells cut underneath. That's how the reel does it.

Use the same grid size on both and the lines sit exactly on top of each other.
