How do I receive future updates?

After purchase, every future update is delivered free to your purchase email. No extra cost.

How do I access the pack after purchase?

Can I use these in monetized videos?

Yes, all animations can be used in personal or commercial projects.

Will this work with my editing software?

Built specifically for Premiere Pro 2019 or newer. No plugins, no After Effects. Everything runs on stock Adobe effects.

What can I customize?

Everything. 52 controls across 11 sections, all inside Premiere's Properties panel. Font, size, colour, position, spacing, speed, direction, drop shadow, stroke, gradient, glow, blur, curve, light sweep, film grain and rough edges. No keyframing.

Do I need any extra plugins?

No - just Premiere Pro. Drag, drop, type. That's it. If you want to save your own looks as reusable presets, build them once in a sequence and import that sequence into your next project. Works on Windows and Mac. On Mac you can also use Clip Deck to do it in one click, though it isn't on Windows yet. The guide in your download covers both.
